# Contributing to Quantum Grid Theory

Thank you for checking out QGT! This is currently a conceptual toy model built on geometric intuition. To advance this framework toward a formal physics paper, we are looking for collaborators to help bridge the remaining gaps.

## Road Map to Sweden (The Nobel Checklist)

1. **Mathematical Formalization:** 
   * Derive the precise tensor equations that define the metric displacement function.
   * Calculate the exact energy density constant required to breach the "Universal Surface Tension" of space.
   
2. **Predictive Proof:**
   * Identify a unique, measurable cosmic signature (such as a specific gravitational wave echo or subatomic quark behavior) that telescopes or particle colliders can search for to prove QGT over standard General Relativity.

## How to Contribute
* **Open an Issue:** If you spot a paradox or want to propose a refinement to the fluid-mechanics analogies.
* **Submit a Pull Request:** If you have mathematical frameworks (e.g., from String Theory or Loop Quantum Gravity) that back up the spatial conservation mechanics.
